Settings

Changes take effect the next time you press a key.

Appearance

How the results page and this page look.

Theme

"Black" uses pure black, ideal on OLED screens. "Like Windows" follows the system's light or dark theme.

Color

The color of the lit key, citations and buttons.

Applies to results and answers to questions.

Reading column width

Shorter lines are easier to read, longer lines scroll less.

Selected text on the side

Shows the text it started from next to the result. You can always open or close it from the page.

Progress near the mouse

A small box tells you what Qwen is doing: searching, reading pages, writing.

Keys

Select text in any program and press the action key. To change it, click the box (or press Enter on it) and press the new key or combination. Esc cancels, Tab leaves the box.

With a screen reader, switch to focus mode (NVDA+Space or JAWS Insert+Z) before pressing the new key.

Fix

Fixes errors and typos in the selected text.

Summarize

Opens the summary in the results page.

Explain

Explains the text and, when needed, searches the web.

Behavior

Where the result appears and how long it is.

Fix directly in the text

In Notepad, Obsidian, Word, VS Code and Notepad++ the fix replaces the selected text. If you turn it off, it opens in the page like the other actions.

Length of summaries and explanations

The fix stays as long as the original text.

Web search

Before explaining, AI-Hotkeys can search the web through SearXNG and read the pages found.

What leaves this PC. SearXNG runs on your PC, but it sends the search words to public search engines (Startpage and Yahoo), and AI-Hotkeys then downloads the pages found from their websites.

The search words come from your selected text. Your full text and Qwen's answer stay on this PC. Choose "Never" below to keep everything local.

When to search

"When needed": searches for technical terms, names, errors; not for personal thoughts or messages.

Search words

"Qwen or from the text": Qwen writes the search; if it writes none, the technical terms or the first 10 words of your selection are searched as they are. "Only Qwen's": only searches written by Qwen are sent, nothing is taken word for word from your selection; without one, the explanation is made without the web.

Results to use

How many sources to pass to Qwen.

Pages to read in full

Qwen only sees the preview of the other sources. More pages give more context but the answer arrives later.
